# Arena beta operations checklist

## Before inviting anyone

- [ ] Dedicated Arena backend project created. No reuse of Shadecode Student data or credentials.
- [ ] Migration applied and tested in staging.
- [ ] RLS tested with at least two ordinary users and one admin.
- [ ] Privacy notice explains collected fields, purpose, retention, deletion and contact route.
- [ ] Support inbox and response owner confirmed.
- [ ] Community rules, anti-cheat policy, moderation and appeal process published.
- [ ] Incident-response owner and escalation path documented.
- [ ] Android/iOS build and supported-version policy recorded.
- [ ] Crash reporting configured without secrets or unnecessary personal data.
- [ ] Closed-beta invitation and consent wording reviewed.
- [ ] No prize, entry-fee, wallet or payout UI is exposed.

## Closed cohort

- [ ] Invite a small, diverse cohort.
- [ ] Track onboarding completion and first-game completion.
- [ ] Check crash-free sessions, failed sessions and replay-verification failures.
- [ ] Collect feedback after first session and after one week.
- [ ] Review all high/blocking reports daily.
- [ ] Interview a sample of players, including people who stop using the app.

## Community beta

- [ ] Expand only after blockers are fixed.
- [ ] Publish free, non-prize event calendar.
- [ ] Test event discovery, registration, capacity and cancellation.
- [ ] Track repeat event participation and day-1/day-7/day-30 retention.
- [ ] Publish known issues and release notes.

## Commercial validation

- [ ] Build a prospect list of schools, clubs, communities, brands and event organisers.
- [ ] Conduct problem interviews before pitching a solution.
- [ ] Record lead source, customer problem, budget owner, expected budget, decision date and next action.
- [ ] Test fixed-fee tournament operations and sponsor packages as separate propositions.
- [ ] Distinguish verbal interest, qualified opportunity, signed agreement and cash received.
- [ ] Do not accept prize funding or run prize-bearing events during this beta.

## Weekly decision review

Review:
- Active players and cohort retention.
- Completed games per active player.
- Event registration-to-completion rate.
- Crash-free sessions and support backlog.
- Fair-play reports and verification failures.
- Qualified organisation/sponsor leads and proposals.
- Signed commitments and cleared revenue actually received.
- Estimated delivery cost and contribution margin.

Keep the beta small if support, reliability or fairness cannot keep up with growth.
